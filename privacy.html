Future Makers

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 July 2026

Future Makers ("the Service", "we", "us") is an internal business messaging tool operated by Confidence Group / GOGAS. This policy explains what information the Service handles and how it is used. The Service is intended for authorised organisational users only and is not offered to the general public.

1. Information We Handle

To deliver scheduled WhatsApp instructions to field teams, the Service stores:

  • Employee contact details entered by authorised administrators (name, employee code, department, and phone number).
  • Message content, attached files, and scheduling settings created by authorised users.
  • Message delivery information (sent, delivered, read status) and replies returned by the WhatsApp Business Platform.
  • Account information for authorised users (name, email, and role).

2. How Information Is Used

Information is used solely to:

  • Send scheduled instructions and reminders to the intended recipients over WhatsApp.
  • Track delivery, read status, and replies so administrators can confirm messages were received.
  • Authenticate users and control access based on their role.

We do not sell, rent, or share this information with third parties for advertising or marketing.

3. WhatsApp Business Platform

The Service sends messages through the WhatsApp Business Platform provided by Meta. Delivery of messages and receipt of replies are subject to Meta's own terms and privacy practices. Recipient phone numbers are used only to deliver the messages requested by authorised administrators.

4. Data Storage & Security

Data is stored in a secured cloud database with access restricted to the Service's backend. Access to the application requires authenticated login. Administrators are responsible for ensuring recipients are aware of, and have agreed to receive, messages from their organisation.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

Contact lists, campaigns, and delivery records are retained for as long as they are needed for operational purposes. Authorised administrators can edit or delete contacts, lists, and campaigns at any time from within the Service. To request deletion of specific data, contact the address below.

6. Contact

For any questions about this policy or requests relating to data, contact: confidencefuturemakers@gmail.com
